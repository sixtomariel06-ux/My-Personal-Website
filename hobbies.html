THINGS THAT MAKE ME HAPPY ♡

My Hobbies and Interests

Everyone has different hobbies and interests that make life more enjoyable. For me, I love doing things that make me happy, help me relax, and allow me to create wonderful memories. Here are some of the activities and interests that I enjoy.

♡ 🌷 ♡

🏸 Sports and Physical Activities

I enjoy playing badminton, especially when having friendly games with my friends. I like how it allows us to stay active while having fun together. It's not always about winning; sometimes, it's simply about enjoying the game, sharing laughs, and spending quality time with one another.

🎧 Music and Favorite Genres

I love listening to music because it helps me relax, improve my mood, and express my feelings. I listen to different kinds of music, but K-pop is one of my favorites. My favorite K-pop group is BTS. I enjoy their songs, performances, and the messages behind their music. Music is something I turn to whenever I want to feel relaxed, entertained, or motivated.

🎬 Movies and Television Series

I love watching movies and Korean dramas during my free time. I enjoy different genres, including adventure, comedy, drama, horror, thriller, science fiction (Sci-Fi), fantasy, romance, mystery, and historical stories.

I like exploring different kinds of stories because each genre offers something unique. Sometimes, I enjoy watching comedy movies when I want to laugh, while other times, I prefer mystery or thriller stories that keep me curious about what will happen next. I also enjoy K-dramas because of their interesting storylines, characters, and emotional moments.

🍜 Food and Culinary Interests

I love trying and eating different kinds of food. Some of my favorites include seafood, spaghetti, and Korean dishes. I enjoy exploring different flavors and discovering foods that I haven't tried before.

Aside from eating, I also enjoy cooking. I find it interesting to prepare food, experiment with ingredients, and learn new recipes. For me, cooking is not just about making something to eat; it's also a fun way to explore my interests and express my creativity.

✈️ Travel, Photography, and Spending Time with Friends

I love hanging out with my friends and visiting different places. Whether it's going somewhere new, exploring interesting spots, eating together, or simply spending time with one another, I enjoy every opportunity to create memorable experiences.

I also enjoy taking photographs whenever I visit places or experience something special. I like capturing little moments because photographs allow me to look back on the memories I've made with the people around me.

I enjoy taking pictures of places, beautiful views, and memorable moments. I like keeping photographs because they remind me of the experiences I've had and the people I've shared them with.

One of the things I enjoy most is spending time with my friends. Whether we're playing badminton, watching movies, eating together, or simply talking about random things, I appreciate these moments. I believe that it's not always about where we go or what we do, but about the people we share those experiences with.

♡ A Little More About My Interests ♡

I enjoy discovering new things, trying different activities, and exploring things that catch my attention. I may have different interests, but they all have one thing in common: they make my everyday life more enjoyable and give me opportunities to learn, experience, and create memories.
